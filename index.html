Drilling Optimisation of PMMA Bone Cement

Aug 2025 – Present

Multivariate Statistical Analysis

  • Designed an L27 full-factorial experiment varying drill diameter, feed rate, and spindle speed; applied ANOVA to decompose variance and identify dominant factors driving heat generation — drill diameter accounted for the largest temperature contribution.
  • Executed Grey Relational Analysis for multi-objective optimisation across correlated response variables (temperature, machining time), achieving a best-case of 56.9°C in 20s — safely below tissue necrosis thresholds.
  • Delivered a parametric guideline by integrating experimental data, statistical modelling, and process-level optimisation — a proof-of-concept for algorithm-driven decision support in physical systems.

Airfoil Shape Optimisation using Genetic Algorithm

Oct 2025 – Jan 2026
  • Implemented a genetic-algorithm optimisation framework in Python — encoding airfoil geometry parameters (thickness, camber, taper, aspect ratio, twist) as a chromosome and evolving populations via selection, crossover, and mutation operators.
  • Integrated Prandtl Lifting Line Theory and a vortex panel method as the fitness evaluation function, combining induced and viscous drag to assess each candidate at a fixed target lift coefficient.
  • Demonstrated convergence to optimised solutions, establishing a lightweight and generalisable supervised-search tool for parametric design — directly applicable to any domain requiring structured parameter-space exploration.

Solar Cell Radiation Tolerance Simulation for Space Applications

Dec 2025 – Present
  • Built a Python post-processing pipeline to parse multi-output simulation data (VACANCY, COLLISION, EXYZ files) from SRIM/TRIM irradiation runs on CsSnI₃ perovskites, generating vacancy density profiles and defect-depth visualisations.
